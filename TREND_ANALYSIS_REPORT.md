## Key Investment Insights

### What Creates Long-term Uptrends?

1. **Technology Leadership:** AAPL, AMZN, GOOG, ANET dominated with 25-40% CAGRs
2. **Network Effects:** Tech platforms that become essential (GOOG, AMZN)
3. **Innovation Moats:** Companies that maintain competitive advantages

- **Volatility:** Average annual volatility was 36.56%

- **Sector Risk:** Airlines and some tech had negative long-term returns
- **Survivorship Bias:** This data only includes current S&P 500 companies

## Methodology

**Metrics Calculated:**
- **CAGR:** Compound Annual Growth Rate - geometric mean return
- **Total Return:** Simple % change from first to last price
- **Momentum Score:** Weighted combination of 6mo (50%), 1yr (30%), 3yr (20%) returns
- **Risk-Adjusted Return:** CAGR divided by annualized volatility (Sharpe-like)
- **Positive Days %:** Percentage of trading days with positive returns
- **Max Drawdown:** Largest peak-to-trough decline

**Data Quality:**
- 30 years of historical data for most stocks
- Daily price data from Yahoo Finance via yfinance
- All returns calculated on adjusted close prices (account for splits/dividends)
